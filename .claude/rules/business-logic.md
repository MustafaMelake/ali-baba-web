# Business-Logic Rules — Pricing, Money, Fulfillment & Time

These are the domain invariants that protect the client's margins and books. They
run through the highest-blast-radius modules in the platform (`src/lib/discounts.ts`,
`placeOrder`, `src/lib/timezone.ts`). Treat every rule here as load-bearing.

## The Discount Engine (`src/lib/discounts.ts`) — one pure resolver, every surface

The engine is a **pure, dependency-free module** (no Prisma, no React) shared by every price surface: shop cards, category cards, `/new-arrivals` cards, collection-page cards (the listing cards all through `src/lib/product-cards.ts`), PDP, homepage badge, homepage hero slides (display-only starting price), search-palette results, logged-in cart hydration, guest re-pricing (also the `/cart` page's live read), wishlist cards — **and `placeOrder`'s billing**. That shared usage is *the* mechanism guaranteeing the shown price equals the billed price. **Never re-implement discount math inside a component** — cards and the PDP call the same functions; the math only ever lives in `discounts.ts`.

The helpers a surface uses:

- `livePromotionWhere(now)` — a Prisma `where` (`isActive && startDate ≤ now ≤ endDate`) spread into every `promotions` include, alongside `PROMOTION_SELECT_FIELDS` for a uniform shape.
- `collectionPromotionsInclude(now)` + `collectionPromotionsOf(product)` — the product-level include for the fourth level and its flattener. The include traverses **only `isActive` collections**, so a draft collection prices nothing; spread it into every product include that prices (all the surfaces above).
- `gatherPromotions(variantPromos, productPromos, categoryPromos, collectionPromos)` — variadic; merges + de-dupes (by id) the four targeting levels, so a promotion reached through several levels or several collections counts once. A promotion applies to a variant if it targets the **variant**, its **parent product**, that product's **category**, or any **active collection** containing the product. A surface that skips the collection level shows a price `placeOrder` won't bill.
- `resolvePrice(basePrice, promos, now)` → `{ basePrice, finalPrice, discountAmount, hasDiscount, appliedPromotion }`.

### "Cheapest Wins" — the formalized overlap rule

When several live promotions apply, **exactly one** applies: the one yielding the **lowest** final price (best for the customer). Promotions **never stack**. There is deliberately **no priority/exclusivity field** — don't add one. `ProductVariant.compareAtPrice` is a purely **visual** manual "was" price (a strikethrough fallback shown only when no live promotion applies); it is **never an input to the discount math**.

**Advertised percentages round DOWN** — `advertisedPercent` / `percentOff` in `discounts.ts` (homepage badge, promo widget, sale badges). A badge may understate a markdown, never overstate it (`19.6%` reads "19%", not "20%"). Don't format a percentage with `Math.round` in a component.

### The promotion ceiling — at most 90% off (no promotion may make a product free)

The engine floors prices at 0, so a big enough promotion silently gives products away — and a 99.99%-off or 299-off-300 typo bills ~1 EGP. **One ceiling (audit M-7) covers both: `PERCENTAGE ≤ 90`, and `FIXED_AMOUNT ≤ 90%` of the cheapest price it reaches** (`maxFixedDiscount(price)` = 90% floored to the piastre). It lives in `discounts.ts` — `MAX_PERCENT_OFF`, `percentageOverCeiling`, `fixedAmountOverCeiling`, `promotionValueProblem` (the price-independent half, run by **both** the admin `PromotionModal` and `validatePromotion`, so their messages match) and `findOverCeilingPromotion` (the product side). Everything is judged on the 2-dp grid the `Decimal(12,2)` columns store (`199.999` is stored as `200.00`). **Never re-implement the comparison inline.** These helpers validate writes only; they never change what `resolvePrice` bills for a promotion already saved.

Three doors are guarded, through that ONE comparison:

1. **Promotion writes** — `promotions.ts` refuses a `PERCENTAGE` over 90 and a `FIXED_AMOUNT` over 90% of the cheapest **available** variant it targets (directly, via its product, via its category, or via a collection — any collection, active or not, since a draft can be published later) on create, update **and** re-activation. `validatePromotion` rounds the value with `roundMoney` **before** every check and stores the rounded value.
2. **Collection writes** — `collections.ts` refuses publishing a collection, or adding products to an active one, when a live or scheduled `FIXED_AMOUNT` promotion on it would take more than 90% off the cheapest variant brought in.
3. **Product writes** — `createProduct` / `updateProduct` refuse the whole write (before the transaction, no partial write) when a live or scheduled `FIXED_AMOUNT` promotion reaching any orderable variant — via that variant, the product, its **new** category, or any collection it's in — would take more than 90% off it: a cheaper new size, a lowered price, or a move into a promoted category. A product being switched off isn't checked (it isn't sold); switching it back on is.

All three are check-then-write, so a concurrent edit can still slip between them; `placeOrder` is the backstop and refuses any 0 EGP line rather than bill it.

### Single `now` per request — mandatory

Capture `const now = new Date()` **once** per request and pass the *same* instant to both `livePromotionWhere(now)` (the DB filter) and `resolvePrice(…, now)` (the in-memory re-check). This is what stops a promotion expiring mid-render/mid-loop from pricing two lines of one order against different instants. Any new consumer must follow it.

## Money math — `roundMoney` is the 2-dp authority

- All currency rounds through `roundMoney` (`Math.round((n + Number.EPSILON) * 100) / 100`); discounts floor at 0.
- **Order of operations (never reorder):** per-line discount **first** → `subtotal = roundMoney(Σ discounted line × qty)` → `vat = isVatEnabled ? roundMoney(subtotal × vatRate) : 0` (on the **discounted** subtotal) → `deliveryFee` (DELIVERY → `roundMoney(branch.deliveryFee ?? settings.defaultDeliveryFee)`; PICKUP → 0) → `totalAmount = roundMoney(subtotal + deliveryFee + vat)`. **Four rounding points**; nothing off-grid reaches the `Decimal` columns. VAT and delivery never apply to the pre-discount price.
- **One implementation: `orderTotals` in `src/lib/cart-summary.ts`** — `placeOrder`'s bill, the checkout preview and the `/cart` summary (`summarizeCart`) all call it, so they cannot disagree. Never re-derive subtotal/VAT/total inline in a page or action.

## VAT is a residual — not a stored column

Only `subtotal`, `deliveryFee`, and `totalAmount` persist as money. Every receipt derives VAT as `Math.max(0, totalAmount - subtotal - deliveryFee)`. `Order.vatRate` snapshots the **rate** billed (`0` when VAT was off; `null` on orders placed before the snapshot) for the receipt's **label only** — `vatLabel(rate)` → "VAT (14%)", or plain "VAT" for `null`. Never recompute the VAT amount from it, and never label an old receipt with today's `StoreSettings` rate. This guarantees the breakdown always reconciles to the total and lets legacy orders (flat-fee, no-VAT era) render `VAT 0` instead of `NaN`. The invariant `total = subtotal + fee + vat` couples three read sites (`dashboard.ts`, `/my-orders`, the admin order drawer) — changing how VAT is stored means touching all three.

## `placeOrder` — the price-integrity boundary

The most consequential write path. The client sends only `{ variantId, quantity }[]` + fulfillment + contact + a resolved `branchId` — **no price ever crosses the wire.** Server-side, inside one short transaction: batched `findMany({ id: { in: … } })` with the four-level live-promotion hierarchy → in-memory loop that **throws (rolls back the whole order)** on any missing/unavailable variant or parent product, **any line un-orderable at the fulfilling branch** — switched off long-term by head office **or** marked "sold out today" by a MANAGER (`BranchVariantAvailability.isAvailable === false` **or** `soldOutUntil > now`, read in the same batched query — the global flags stay the master switch, a missing row means available; the single request `now` judges the sold-out mark, which expires on its own at the next Cairo midnight), **or any line that resolves to 0 EGP** → `resolvePrice` per line → the discounted `finalPrice` is **snapshotted** onto `OrderItem` → for a signed-in buyer, the ordered lines are deleted from their saved cart **in the same transaction** (the client only empties its local copy). Order preconditions: shared `checkoutSchema` validation (a DELIVERY order **must** carry a non-empty `addressLine`; each `variantId` may appear **once** — a duplicated line is refused, never merged; `customerPhone` must be an Egyptian mobile and is normalized to canonical E.164 by `normalizeEgyptMobile`), a per-phone throttle (**≤ 3 simultaneously-`PENDING` orders per canonical `customerPhone`** — Egypt COD fake-order protection; `010…`, `+2010…`, `+20 010…` and Arabic-Indic digits are one phone, and the invisible direction marks a WhatsApp/contacts copy-paste carries are stripped first — audit L-4). The throttle is **atomic**: its `count` runs inside the order transaction behind `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('order-phone:' + phone, 0))`, so concurrent orders for one phone serialize and the cap holds exactly (a plain pre-count before the transaction is only a fast reject for a phone already at the cap — the locked count is the authority). It does **not** stop number rotation (~400M valid mobiles); that is the per-IP Vercel Firewall rate limit's job (`docs/DEPLOY_RUNBOOK.md`), never a reason to loosen the cap; and **a PICKUP must resolve to a real, active branch** — otherwise it is refused (`PICKUP_UNAVAILABLE_ERROR`) before any pricing work; only DELIVERY keeps the unassigned fallback. Never thread a client price into the payload "for convenience."

**Price consent (`expectedTotal`).** The one money figure the client may send is the total it **displayed** — computed with the same `orderTotals` — as an advisory `expectedTotal`. `placeOrder` bills its own fresh total regardless; it only **compares** (on the 2-dp grid, `sameTotal`) and, on any difference (up or down: a promotion ended, a price, VAT or delivery fee changed while checkout was open), throws inside the transaction **before the order is created** and returns `{ success: false, code: "PRICES_CHANGED", summary }` with the server's fresh subtotal / fee / VAT / total / line prices. The checkout shows `PricesChangedNotice` in place of the CTA; Confirm resubmits with `summary.total` as the new `expectedTotal`. It is never billed, never used as a price, and optional in `checkoutSchema` so a browser on the previous deploy can still order. The shared type and constant live in `src/lib/cart-summary.ts` (`PricesChangedSummary`, `PRICES_CHANGED`).

## Reporting — two formalized rules

0. **Order status moves one step at a time (audit M-3).** Because revenue is whatever the status says, `updateOrderStatus` only allows the steps in `ORDER_STATUS_TRANSITIONS` (`src/lib/order-status.ts`): DELIVERY `PENDING → PREPARING → SHIPPED → DELIVERED`, PICKUP `PENDING → PREPARING → DELIVERED`, `CANCELLED` from any non-final step; **`DELIVERED` and `CANCELLED` are final** (also for an ADMIN). Anything else returns `{ success: false, code: "INVALID_TRANSITION", from, to }`. The write is conditional on the status (and a MANAGER's branch) just read, and stamps `statusChangedAt` + `statusChangedById` — the drawer shows "Last changed by …" and offers only the next steps. Fixing a mis-click on a final status is a deliberate data fix, never a new status click.
1. **Revenue strictly counts `DELIVERED` orders only** — not "non-cancelled." Unconfirmed PENDING/PREPARING/SHIPPED cash is never revenue. Every revenue aggregate, groupBy, and raw rollup (dashboard, branch sales, star-of-month, top products) carries `status: DELIVERED`. **Order-*volume* counters** (today/yesterday, peak hours) deliberately stay status-agnostic (they exclude at most `CANCELLED`) — they measure activity, not money.
2. **Every business "day"/"month" is an `Africa/Cairo` calendar boundary**, expressed as an exact UTC instant. Use `src/lib/timezone.ts` (`STORE_TZ`, `storeMidnight`, `storeMonthStart`, `storeDayKey`) — **DST-safe** (Egypt reinstated DST in 2023; a hardcoded UTC+2 is wrong for part of the year) and **never the Node server's local clock**. Raw-SQL analytics do the equivalent with `AT TIME ZONE`, importing the same `STORE_TZ` so JS and SQL agree on the store day.

## Branches — four hats, soft retirement

A `Branch` is simultaneously a **pickup point**, a **delivery area**, the **per-branch delivery-fee source**, and the **unit of MANAGER RBAC**. Retire with `isActive: false`; deletion is deliberately hard (blocked by `User.branch` Restrict). At checkout the `"Other Areas"` option is a client-side sentinel (`id: "__other__"`) that maps to `branchId: null` (→ unassigned → Super-Admin-only) — **it must never reach the server.** The delivery fee shipped to the client is display-only; `placeOrder` re-reads the fee from the real, active branch row. **Every fee obeys one rule** — `parseDeliveryFee` in `@/lib/validators` (`0 ≤ fee ≤ MAX_DELIVERY_FEE` = 10,000 EGP, rounded to piastres) — through both doors: the branch form (`manage-branches.ts`) and the Settings fee sheet (`store-settings.ts`).

## Cart sync — identity & intent, never money

`CartItem` persists only `{ userId, variantId, quantity }` — no price column; `getDbCartAction` re-resolves price on every read. The store keys everything on `variantId`. A **record-then-confirm `pendingOps` ledger** writes intent to `localStorage` before each logged-in sync leaves and clears it only on exact-match confirmation, so a failed sync replays on the next hydrate. `CartSyncProvider` distinguishes **hydrate** (already-logged-in on mount — plain adopt, never sum) from the **one true merge** (guest → logged-in — server SUMs, clamped). A cart is merged into an account **at most once, across tabs**: the store persists `ownerId` (the account the local cart mirrors; null for a guest), claims it before merging, and skips the merge — hydrating instead — for a cart the account already owns; the provider re-reads storage before merging and on every other-tab write. (Tabs share localStorage but not memory, and Better Auth refetches the session on tab focus.) Shared limits from `@/lib/validators`: `CHECKOUT_MAX_QUANTITY = 99` (per line) and `CHECKOUT_MAX_ITEMS = 50` (distinct lines, enforced by the store, the cart actions, `checkoutSchema`, and the DB-cart cap alike).

## Reviews

Auth-gated (no anonymous path); `userId` + `authorName` come from the session, **never** form input (`authorName` is a point-in-time snapshot). Created `isApproved: false` — invisible until an admin approves. Moderation revalidates the queue always, and the public PDP only when the change affects it.
